Use case: logo-brand.

Input image 1: STYLE REFERENCE ONLY. It shows the final Knight horse, Assault eagle, and Ghost tiger. Do not redraw or include these three existing emblems. Generate ONLY the new Point lion emblem.

PRIMARY REQUEST
Design one highly polished original left-facing LION head and short mane/neck bust emblem in the precise visual language of the reference family. The lion represents Point, a female British close-range breacher. Use a heraldic male lion's mane as a national/animal symbol, not a portrait of the female character.

GAMEPLAY TO INFORM THE MARK
Point alternates an SMG and a shotgun without losing firepower: the holstered gun reloads itself. Her Gun Kata is fluid rapid switching and accurate moving fire; her Breach ultimate uses a nine-bang flash grenade to open a path through heavy enemies. The defining visual idea is fluid continuous close-range firepower.
Make the LION read first, and integrate a subtle pair of weapon cues INTO THE MANE, as elegantly as the negative-space sword, bullet trails, and knife/reticle integrate into the three approved icons. Do not put generic crossed guns underneath a lion.

DESIGN
- One proud, fierce, unmistakable lion in LEFT-FACING side profile. Short broad feline muzzle, pronounced brow, one small angular triangular negative-space eye, a small round ear set inside a full powerful mane.
- Differentiate from the tiger reference with a substantial flowing mane framing the back and lower neck, no tiger stripes. Lion facial anatomy, not a wolf, dragon, horse, or generic panther.
- A slightly open compact angular mouth suggesting the start of a forceful roar; keep the face extremely reduced, avoid rows of teeth. A strong jaw/chin and muzzle, one tiny fang only if necessary.
- Use 3 or 4 LARGE, confident sweeping mane locks, broad continuous WHITE masses separated by a few broad, purposeful BLACK channels. Every sweeping curve resolves in a clean decisive sharp point; no fiddly fur.
- Within the lower two mane channels, integrate TWO compact bold BLACK weapon-like silhouettes flowing with the mane: an understated short SMG barrel/receiver and a short pump-shotgun barrel/fore-end. Both orient toward the lion's forward direction (left), using a few blocky/clipped transitions so each reads as a weapon cue rather than a hairline. Their swept rear ends merge naturally into the mane motion. This should read as ONE cohesive lion emblem, not a collage of a head and guns.
- Keep the weapon cues restrained enough that the silhouette remains clear at 48px.
- Short strong shoulder/bust terminates in a broad slightly rising-to-the-right wedge base, matching the general bust footprint of the family.
- Convey speed, control and forward entry. Shape language should feel precise and elegant as well as forceful.
- No crown or heraldic shield, Union Jack, lettering, circular frame, floating ammunition, flash star, nine dots, ornamental filigree, or extra insignia.

STYLE AND COMPOSITION
A single solid WHITE emblem with BLACK cutouts on a pure solid BLACK square background. Flat two-tone vector-like graphic, with absolutely NO grey shading, lighting, gradients, texture, glow, borders, drop shadows, 3D effect, or mockup.
Match the reference's visual weight, large uninterrupted white masses, flowing concave cuts, sharp angled eye, few purposeful detail cuts, and asymmetric left-profile bust layout.
Centered one icon, fills approximately 80 percent of square width and height, generous safe black padding on all sides. Refined optically balanced original finished class emblem. No text, labels, grid, alternatives, other icons, or watermark.
